Step-by-Step Implementation Guide

Follow these 9 easy steps to fully automate your sales commission process.

Step 1: Install the module

Navigate to the Apps menu, search for "Profit-Based Tiered Sales Commission" and click Install.

Step 2: Create a commission plan

Go to Sales Commission → Configuration → Commission Plans and click Create.

Step 3: Define tier rules

Configure the From/To Amount and Rate (%). Supports Flat or Marginal logic.

Step 4: Set product cost

For Gross Profit basis, ensure products have their Cost price set to compute COGS.

Step 5: Post a customer invoice

Post an invoice with an assigned Salesperson. An "Accrued" entry is created automatically.

Step 6: Register payment

Once paid, the commission status moves to Payable.

Step 7: Review commission ledger

Monitor all earnings in Sales Commission → Commission Ledger.

Step 8: Manage settlement periods

Group entries into Settlement Periods and lock them for payout.

Step 9: Sales portal

Sales reps can view their earnings in real-time via the Customer Portal.
