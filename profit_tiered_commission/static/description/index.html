Step-by-Step Implementation Guide

Follow these 9 easy steps to fully automate your sales commission process.

1. Install the module

Step 01

Navigate to the Apps menu, search for "Profit-Based Tiered Sales Commission" and click the Install button.

2. Create a commission plan

Step 02

Go to Sales Commission → Configuration → Commission Plans. Click Create to start a new plan for your team.

3. Define tier rules

Step 03

Configure the From/To Amount and Rate (%). You can choose between "Flat" (entire amount at one rate) or "Marginal" (different rates for each range) logic.

4. Set product cost

Step 04

If you use Gross Profit as the basis, ensure all products have their Cost price correctly set in the product form to compute COGS.

5. Post a customer invoice

Step 05

Create a standard Customer Invoice. Assign a Salesperson who is linked to a commission plan. Upon clicking Post, an "Accrued" ledger entry is created.

6. Register payment

Step 06

Once the customer pays the invoice and you Register Payment, the system automatically moves the commission status to Payable.

7. Review commission ledger

Step 07

Monitor all earnings in Sales Commission → Commission Ledger. You can see the breakdown of Net Sales, Gross Profit, and the final Commission Amount.

8. Manage settlement periods

Step 08

Group entries into monthly or quarterly Settlement Periods. Review the totals and click Lock to freeze the data for payout processing.

9. Sales portal

Step 09

Sales representatives can log in to the Customer Portal and view their personal commission history, current earnings, and status of each sale in real-time.
