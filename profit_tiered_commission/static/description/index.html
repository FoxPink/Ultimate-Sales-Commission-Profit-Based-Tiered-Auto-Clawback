Profit-Based Tiered Sales Commission

Automated ledger • tiered plans • auto-clawback

Why choose this module?

Replace manual spreadsheets with a transparent, automated commission workflow.

[ Ledger ]

Accrued → Payable

Follows invoice payment status automatically. Entries move from "Accrued" to "Payable" when paid.

[ Clawback ]

Auto-Clawback

Credit notes automatically generate negative commission entries, ensuring accurate payouts.

[ Tiered ]

Tiered Plans

Configure multiple tiers based on Revenue or Gross Profit. Supports flat and marginal rates.

Quick Start Guide

1
Create Plan: Go to Sales Commission → Configuration → Commission Plans.
2
Define Tiers: Set ranges (From/To) and rates (%). Leave "To" empty for the top tier.
3
Assign Costs: Ensure products have "Cost" set if using Gross Profit basis.
4
Process Sales: Post invoices with a salesperson assigned to generate accrued ledger.
5
Settle: Register payment to move entries to Payable for final settlement.

Detailed Configuration

Follow these steps to master the commission workflow.

Step 1

Configure Commission Plans

  • • Plan Type: Choose between Revenue or Gross Profit based commission.
  • • Product Categories: Optionally limit plans to specific categories.
  • • Tier Logic: Configure flat rates or progressive tiers.
Step 2

Define Tier Rules

  • • From/To Amount: Defines the range for each tier.
  • • Commission Rate (%): The percentage earned within this tier.
  • • Top Tier: Use 0 or leave empty in "To Amount" for the final tier.
Step 3

Commission Calculation Logic

1. Basis Selection

Choose how you want to reward your team:

  • Revenue Basis: Commission is calculated on the Net Sales amount (Sales - Discounts).
  • Gross Profit Basis: Commission is calculated on the Profit (Sales - COGS). Requires product cost configuration.
2. Tier Application

The system automatically identifies the correct rate:

  • Flat Tier: The highest reached tier's rate applies to the entire amount.
  • Marginal Tier: Different rates apply to each portion of the amount within each tier.
3. Automated Lifecycle
Invoice Posted → Accrued Payment Received → Payable Refund Issued → Clawback
Step 4

Sales & Payout Lifecycle

Accrual Phase

When an invoice is posted, commission is "Accrued" but not yet payable.

Payable Phase

Once payment is registered, entries move to "Payable" automatically.

Clawback Logic

Credit notes trigger negative entries linked to the original commission.

Portal Access

Salespeople can view their own real-time performance on the portal.
